Puzzle Pups

Word puzzles for iPad with big, clear letters and five friendly dogs.

Common questions

How do I make the writing bigger?

Tap Settings on the home screen, then choose a size under Reading. Puzzle Pups also follows your iPad's own text size: if that's set bigger, the app uses at least the matching size.

How do I play each game?

Each game shows a short "How to play" card the first time. To see it again, tap the ? button at the top of the puzzle.

Can I use an Apple Pencil?

Yes, but you don't need one. In a word search you can drag across a word, tap its first and last letters, or draw a ring round it. In the other games you can write answers by hand. Everything also works with a finger.

I missed a day. Have I lost my streak?

Not necessarily. For every seven days you play, the dogs save up a nap that covers a missed day. You can also open Today's Puzzles, scroll down to the calendar, and catch up on a day you missed.

Can more than one person play?

Yes. In Settings → Family players, tap Add a family member. Everyone gets their own levels, stars and trophies, and Rankings shows how everyone's going.

How do I make a puzzle from our own words?

In Settings → Family puzzles, tap Make a family puzzle, then type at least eight words with a clue for each, such as grandchildren's names or favourite places. It then appears in every game except the Word Wheel.

Does it need the internet?

No. Puzzle Pups works anywhere, with no account and no ads.

Will I lose my progress on a new iPad?

Your progress is kept on the iPad and included in its iCloud or computer backup. Set up the new iPad from that backup and your levels, trophies and family puzzles come with it.

How do I start again?

Settings → Start again → Reset my progress clears the current player's levels, stars and trophies. It can't be undone.

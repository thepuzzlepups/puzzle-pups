Privacy policy

Puzzle Pups doesn't collect any information about you.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What the app keeps, and where

Puzzle Pups saves the names you type for each player, your progress, trophies and settings, and any family puzzles you make. All of it stays in the app on your iPad. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Like the rest of your iPad, it's included in your iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. Puzzle Pups has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. We don't use any third-party services that could collect information.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't identify you. You can turn this off at any time.

Children

Puzzle Pups is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

To remove one player, use Settings → Family players in the app. To remove everything, delete the app from your iPad.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the new version of the app is released.
